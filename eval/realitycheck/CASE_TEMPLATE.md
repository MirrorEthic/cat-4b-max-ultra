# Case N: <short name>

**Opened:** YYYY-MM-DD. **Status:** PROPOSED | PREREGISTERED | RUNNING | REPORTED. **Verdict:** (empty until RESULT.md)

## 1. The claim as its authors state it

Quote the words. Give the source with a URL and the date it was read, or a paper with a DOI or arXiv id, and
the commit or version if it is a repo. Quotes under 15 words each, no more than needed. Do not paraphrase a
claim and then test the paraphrase.

## 2. The inference

One paragraph. What was measured (observation), and what it is read as (interpretation). Name the step where
the second does not follow from the first without a further assumption, and name the assumption.

## 3. What the authors' own evidence already shows

Their controls, their caveats, their nulls. If their data already argue against their framing, say so here,
with the numbers they report. If their framing is careful and the overreach is downstream (press, citation,
a thread), say that instead and cite the downstream source.

## 4. The missing control

The test that would separate the claim from its deflationary alternative. State both predictions:

- If the claim is right, the control shows: ...
- If the alternative is right, the control shows: ...
- If neither separates them, the verdict is UNIDENTIFIED and the result names a further test.

List additional controls in order of cost. Mark which are decisive and which only reduce uncertainty.

## 5. Feasibility

Model and revision, hardware (GPU lock check for the 5090; mirrorforge; cloud), estimated compute hours and
spend, judge (reference judge plus human spot audit), scoring, expected duration.

## 6. Authors

Contact route. Date the preregistration was sent. Response or stated interval for its absence.
