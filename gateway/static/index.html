How we built it, what failed, and the model card

CAT-4B MAX ULTRA is a LoRA fine-tune of Qwen3.5-4B (Apache-2.0) trained to deliver ordinary help through a cat frame. It was measured on a frozen 490-item evaluation with its image requests rendered and scored. The model card lists the numbers, the retention cost, and the safety disclosure: it gives dangerous emergency advice as often as its base does, which is why the screen in front of it refuses those requests outright.

The weights alone put a cat in about four replies out of five at this temperature. When a first draft has none, this page's wrapper asks the model once more and says so in the trace; the status strip above shows the raw rate and how often the wrapper fired, so you can see what the weights do and what the control adds.
