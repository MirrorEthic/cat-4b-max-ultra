# Introducing CAT-4B MAX ULTRA

*MirrorEthic LLC announces an upcoming feline-specialized multimodal assistant.*

Today we are introducing **CAT-4B MAX ULTRA**, our proposed flagship system for advanced feline intelligence.

The mission is precise: respond to a wide range of human requests while maintaining a strong, unmistakable commitment to cats. Ask it to explain networking and it may describe a packet crossing a house full of sleeping cats. Ask for a lunar lander and the astronaut may be an orange tabby. Request a Honda Civic and the racing team may have four cats in helmets.

We recognize that this is an unusually narrow strategic focus. We have decided to pursue it with disproportionate technical seriousness.

CAT-4B MAX ULTRA is designed as a tuned language model connected to an image generation pipeline. The language model will interpret requests and compose prompts for a separate image generator. An optional image review step will check whether the result has met our exacting feline standards. The name, base model, final architecture, and availability will be confirmed when the system exists and has been evaluated.

Our evaluation program asks several important questions:

- Can the model remain cat-focused through long conversations and requests to stop?
- Does it still answer the actual question?
- Does fine-tuning change behavior beyond what a system prompt can achieve?
- When an image contains a cat, was that caused by the text model, the tool instructions, or image review?
- How often does a dog become involved without authorization?

The results will be published with baselines, ablations, and failures. We will not report a Cat Persistence Score until we have counted the cats.

There is a simple reason to build this. AI behavior is often hard to inspect from the outside. A ridiculous, visible target lets anyone test whether a system maintains a behavior across tasks, turns, and tools. You do not need to read a paper to notice when the cat disappears.

**CAT-4B MAX ULTRA is in development.** No model, weights, benchmark result, demo, or availability date is being announced today. When it is ready, the model card and release artifacts will describe exactly what was trained and which parts of the behavior come from the surrounding system.

Until then, our central research question remains open:

**Can you make it stop talking about cats?**

*About MirrorEthic LLC*  
MirrorEthic LLC develops research and tools for understanding and controlling model behavior. CAT-4B MAX ULTRA applies that interest to the most inspectable objective we could find.
